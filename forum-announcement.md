## Title: Ivory Garden — a warm paper-light recolour of Minimal

## Body

Hi everyone 👋

I'd like to share **Ivory Garden** — a new theme I've been working on. It's a recolour of [@kepano](https://forum.obsidian.md/u/kepano)'s wonderful Minimal theme, designed for people who spend long hours reading and writing in Obsidian.

### The problem it solves

Most light themes default to pure white backgrounds with pure black text. After a few hours, that's harsh on the eyes. I wanted something softer — a theme that feels more like reading a printed book than staring at a screen.

### The palette

Ivory Garden uses:

- **Ivory paper backgrounds** (`#FAF5F0`) instead of pure white — less blue light, less fatigue
- **Deep cocoa text** (`#3E2723`) instead of pure black — same readability, softer contrast
- **A single cobalt blue accent** (`#0B68DA`) for links and interactive elements — cool enough to stand out against the warm paper
- **A signature muted gold glow** (`#C49A3C`) for text highlights — like sunlight through bokeh, not a highlighter pen

### Dark mode: Midnight Garden

The dark variant inverts the same palette — espresso-black backgrounds with warm ivory text, a lightened cobalt accent, and a brighter gold glow that survives the darkness. I call it Midnight Garden.

### What's included

Because Ivory Garden is a Minimal recolour, you inherit the full Minimal feature set:

- Focus mode, cards, image grid, table helpers, tab styles, callouts, embeds
- Plugin compatibility: Calendar, Charts, Dataview, Git, Kanban, Style Settings, Zoom
- The complete Style Settings surface for granular customization
- All 14 of Minimal's preset color schemes (Dracula, Gruvbox, Nord, etc.) remain selectable

### Install

Once approved, you can install it from **Settings → Appearance → Themes → Manage** by searching for "Ivory Garden".

For manual install or to look at the source: https://github.com/W-O-Debian/ivory-garden

### Credits

All credit for the theme architecture, feature set, plugin compatibility, and Style Settings surface goes to [@kepano](https://forum.obsidian.md/u/kepano). Minimal is one of the best-designed themes in the Obsidian ecosystem, and Ivory Garden wouldn't exist without it. I only changed the colors — everything else is Steph's work.
